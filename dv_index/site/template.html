Who a law reaches

Every bill here affects DV survivors. These realms narrow the list to particular survivors. A bill joins a realm only when its own text uses that realm's language, so some realms are small.

No 2025–26 bill names a religion, an ethnicity or a South Asian language, so bills can't be sorted by community. For Muslim and South Asian survivors, the realms below that advocates most often raise are immigration and VAWA, language access, faith, and forced marriage (under Women & gender). None of these issues is unique to those communities.

How bills are flagged

Source

The Legislature's official bulk data (downloads.leginfo.legislature.ca.gov). For each AB and SB bill, the tool reads the latest version's title, subject and Legislative Counsel's Digest.

Direct

The bill names domestic, intimate partner, dating or family violence, or cites a section of the Domestic Violence Prevention Act (Family Code §§6200–6409).

Indirect

The bill touches an impact area (housing, immigration, custody, firearms and so on) and uses victim or safety language. Some citations count on their own, such as Civil Code §1946.7, which lets victims end a lease.
